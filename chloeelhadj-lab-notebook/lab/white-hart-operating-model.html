Case Study · Jul 2026

The White Hart Operating Model

Boutique hotels create value by transforming information into coordinated human decisions. This project explores how organizational knowledge can be modeled before introducing AI.

From Model to Practice

The operating model above is abstract by design. What follows makes it concrete: a daily-use dashboard, the technical layer beneath it, one ticket traced through the whole system, a capture tool for informal knowledge, and the closing image of what organizational memory actually looks like.

Key Insights

The front desk is the hotel's real coordination hub · most friction is a handoff problem, not a technology gap · institutional knowledge is the true differentiator · AI's role is retrieval, not decision-making · the dashboard is one visualization of the operating model, not the project itself

Reflection

Boutique hospitality depends on coordinated human intelligence. Technology's role here isn't to replace that — it's to preserve and strengthen it.
