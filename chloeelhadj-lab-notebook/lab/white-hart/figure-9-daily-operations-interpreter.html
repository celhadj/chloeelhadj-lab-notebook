Prototype — Institutional Knowledge Capture

Organized output from this tool is what populates the Institutional Knowledge Feed in Figure 7.

Daily Operations Interpreter

Write today's notes the way you'd actually think them. This organizes what you wrote — it doesn't manage anything for you.

This is not a property management system. It does not track reservations, bookings, or billing. It exists for one narrow purpose: capturing the informal, undocumented knowledge that currently only lives in one person's head — the exact gap this case study identified — and organizing it into a form someone else could actually use.

Nothing typed here is saved or sent anywhere beyond this one request — there's no database behind this, intentionally. The point is to show what becomes visible when informal notes get structured, not to build a place to store real operational data.
