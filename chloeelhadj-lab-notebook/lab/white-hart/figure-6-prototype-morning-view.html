The Morning View — Hotel

What a manager or front-desk agent might open at 7:00 a.m. Scoped to hotel operations specifically — restaurant and café business appears only where it involves a hotel guest.

This is a conceptual mockup, not a functioning product or a real data feed. Every name, room number, and note below is illustrative — invented to show the shape of the idea, not drawn from actual guest or operational records. Every panel is built to answer a friction point already documented in this case study, not to demonstrate "everything a hotel could show."
