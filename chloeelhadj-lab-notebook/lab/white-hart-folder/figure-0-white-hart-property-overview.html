The White Hart

Salisbury, Connecticut — the property's components, loosely arranged for orientation only.

A minimal site sketch, not an architectural survey — proportions and adjacencies are illustrative.

The White Hart is an ideal case study because it combines several distinct hospitality businesses within a single property. On any given day, the organization simultaneously operates as a boutique inn, full-service restaurant, neighborhood tap room, provisions market, and event venue while coordinating housekeeping, maintenance, accounting, purchasing, and guest services behind the scenes. Each operation has its own staff, workflows, priorities, and information needs, yet all contribute to a single guest experience.

This complexity makes the White Hart representative of a broader class of organizations whose competitive advantage depends less on manufacturing products than on coordinating people, information, and judgment. Rather than examining hospitality through the lens of buildings or departments, this project investigates the hotel as an operating system — one in which information is continuously created, shared, interpreted, and transformed into personalized service.
