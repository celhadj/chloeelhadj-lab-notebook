Design Principles

Not software. The values behind every decision in this project.

1. Information loses value when it is repeatedly re-entered, copied, or reconstructed across systems and departments.
2. Organizational knowledge should outlast individual employees. Guest preferences, property quirks, operational lessons, and vendor relationships should become organizational assets rather than personal memory.
3. AI should retrieve context — not make decisions. Technology should surface the right information at the right moment while leaving judgment, empathy, and accountability to people.
4. Human coordination creates hospitality. Exceptional guest experiences emerge from well-coordinated teams, not isolated decisions. Better information enables better collaboration across departments.
5. Every interaction should make the organization smarter. Reservations, maintenance requests, guest feedback, and completed events should continuously strengthen the hotel's institutional memory.

These principles guided every prototype and workflow presented in this case study.
